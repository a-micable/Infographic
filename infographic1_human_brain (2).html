Remarkable Facts
01
The brain generates about 20 watts of power — enough to power a dim light bulb continuously while you think.
02
Your brain is 73% water. Being dehydrated by just 2% impairs attention, memory, and motor skills.
03
Neurons fire at speeds up to 120 m/s — signals travel from your brain to your toes in under a second.
04
The brain has no pain receptors. Brain surgery can be performed while a patient is awake and talking.
05
Memory capacity is virtually unlimited — estimated at 2.5 petabytes, enough to store 3 million hours of TV.
